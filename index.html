FAQ

Quick answers.

Is the secret shopper legal?

Yes. We call publicly listed business numbers during business hours, identify the purpose in follow-up, and record in line with applicable laws. Businesses opt in by signing up.

Do I need new hardware or phone systems?

No. YEW works with the phone number you already have. Nothing to install.

What about spam laws for the texts?

Revive only texts leads who previously contacted your business, includes opt-out on every message, and honors STOP instantly. We guide you through carrier registration.

I'm a freelancer, not a big company. Does this work for me?

Absolutely — solo operators miss more calls than anyone. One recovered client usually covers a year of YEW.
