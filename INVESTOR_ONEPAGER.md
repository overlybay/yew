# YEW — Investor One-Pager

## The problem

Local service businesses are bleeding money in two places nobody watches:

1. **Missed and mishandled calls.** Roughly a third of inbound calls to small
   service businesses go unanswered or end badly — long holds, no greeting,
   straight to voicemail. Every one of those is a $200–$2,000 job walking to
   a competitor, and the owner never hears about it.
2. **Dead lead lists.** Every contractor, dentist, and salon sits on hundreds
   of old quotes and inquiries that went cold. That list cost real ad money
   to build — and then it rots in a spreadsheet, worth $0.

## The solution — YEW

An AI customer-recovery platform with two engines:

- **YEW Audit — the AI secret shopper.** Once a week, an AI calls the
  business posing as a customer and scores the whole experience: answered?
  time-to-answer? greeting quality? hold time? outcome? The owner gets a
  scorecard with the recording, transcript, and an estimate of revenue lost
  that week. Problems become visible — and fixable — for the first time.
- **YEW Revive — dead-lead resurrection.** The business uploads its cold lead
  list; YEW runs a 3-message SMS drip (day 0, 4, 10) written for their trade.
  Every reply lands in a dashboard inbox, flagged the second it happens.
  Old quotes turn back into booked jobs.

## Product status

Working SaaS, live today: business dashboard, weekly audit scorecards with
recordings and transcripts, Revive campaign builder with CSV upload, live SMS
inbox with reply alerts, and automatic STOP/opt-out compliance. Built on
Twilio (voice/SMS) + Supabase + Vercel — infrastructure cost per customer is
$3–8/month.

## Business model

- **YEW Audit** — $49/mo (weekly secret-shopper calls + scorecards)
- **YEW Revive** — $79/mo (dead-lead SMS resurrection)
- **Complete bundle** — $99/mo
- **Pay-per-booking option** — $25 per booked appointment (land-and-expand
  wedge: prove ROI first, convert to subscription later)

Gross margin ~90%+. Net revenue retention should be strong: once an owner
sees recovered jobs attributed to YEW, churn becomes expensive for *them*.

## Market

Millions of US local service businesses — contractors (plumbing, HVAC,
roofing, electrical), dental practices, salons/spas, auto repair — overwhelmingly
owner-operated, underserved by modern software, and already spending on ads
to generate the very leads YEW recovers. Serviceable initial niche: home
services + dental + salon in major metros. A 10,000-customer footprint at
~$80 ARPU is a ~$10M ARR business; the category extends nationally from there.

## Why now

Voice AI and SMS automation just got cheap enough for the small-business
price point — a weekly AI phone audit cost ~$5 to run two years ago; today
it's cents. The mystery-shopping industry is still human, slow, and
enterprise-priced. The dead-lead follow-up tools that exist are built for
large sales teams, not a two-truck plumbing shop. The window is open now.

## Moat

- **Proprietary audit data:** every call builds a scored dataset of real
  SMB phone interactions — greeting quality, hold patterns, close rates —
  segmented by trade. That dataset gets smarter and harder to replicate
  with every customer.
- **Revival playbooks per trade:** message sequences tuned by vertical
  (roofing ≠ dental), refined by actual reply and booking rates.
- **Embedded workflow:** scorecards + inbox become the owner's weekly habit;
  ripping YEW out means going blind again.

## The ask

Raising a small angel round to scale what already works: fund direct sales
to local businesses, automate onboarding end-to-end, and expand the audit
scoring from heuristic grading to full AI-graded scorecards with
trade-specific benchmarks.
